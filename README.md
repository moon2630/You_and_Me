## 📖 About You & Me

**You & Me** is a multi-module Android e-commerce application containing two separate apps inside one project:

- 👤 **User App** – Browse and purchase products
- 🛍️ **Seller App** – Add products and manage sales

Both apps use the same **Firebase database** and shared model classes for real-time data management.

---

## 👤 User App Features

### 🔐 Authentication
Create an account, log in and recover a forgotten password.

### 🏠 Home Dashboard
View offers, brands, recently viewed products and all available products.

### 🛒 Product Shopping
View product images, prices, colours, sizes, delivery details, ratings and reviews.

### ❤️ Wishlist and Cart
Save favourite products, add items to the cart and view the complete price breakdown.

### 💳 Checkout
Change the delivery address, select a dummy payment method and confirm the order.

### 📦 Orders and Tracking
Track orders, cancel within two days and return products within seven days after delivery.

### 🔍 Smart Search
Search products using text or voice and apply filters such as brand, colour, price and discount.

## 🛍️ Seller App Features

### 🔐 Seller Authentication
Create an account, log in and complete email verification.

### 📝 Seller Setup
Add seller information, address, bank details, PAN, Aadhaar and agreement details.

### 📊 Seller Dashboard
View income, products, pending orders, sales statistics and monthly charts.

### ➕ Add Products
Select a category, upload images and enter product, stock and manufacturer details.

### 📦 Manage Products
View, update, delete and sort products with low-stock alerts.

### 🚚 Order Management
View customer orders and update their order status.

### 💰 Billing and Earnings
View product prices, company commission, fees and total earnings.
